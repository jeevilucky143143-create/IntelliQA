Education is the process of facilitating learning, or the acquisition of knowledge, skills, values, morals, beliefs, habits, and personal development. Educational methods include teaching, training, storytelling, discussion, and directed research.

Higher education, also called post-secondary education, includes undergraduate and postgraduate education offered by universities, colleges, and institutes of technology. Higher education fosters advanced research, critical thinking, and professional specialization across humanities, sciences, and engineering.

Educational technology (EdTech) refers to the combined use of computer hardware, software, and educational theory to facilitate learning and improve performance. Modern EdTech incorporates online learning platforms, virtual classrooms, intelligent tutoring systems, and gamified learning applications.

Distance learning and online education enable students to acquire degrees and certifications remotely through digital portals. Platforms like Khan Academy, Coursera, edX, and Udemy provide open educational resources to millions of learners globally.

STEM education focuses on an interdisciplinary curriculum comprising Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics. STEM programs emphasize hands-on problem solving, computational logic, and experiential learning to prepare students for modern technical careers.

Bloom's Taxonomy is a widely adopted pedagogical framework created by Benjamin Bloom in 1956. It classifies cognitive educational objectives into six hierarchical levels: Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, and Create.
