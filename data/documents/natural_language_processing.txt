Natural Language Processing (NLP) is an interdisciplinary subfield of computer science, artificial intelligence, and linguistics concerned with the interactions between computers and human language.

Key tasks in natural language processing include tokenization, part-of-speech tagging, named entity recognition (NER), sentiment analysis, machine translation, syntactic parsing, and question answering systems.

Traditional NLP methods relied heavily on rule-based systems and statistical language modeling, using techniques such as N-grams, TF-IDF vectorization, Hidden Markov Models, and Naive Bayes classifiers.

Modern NLP utilizes deep learning and transformer architectures. Pretrained language models like BERT, RoBERTa, T5, and GPT have revolutionized NLP by learning contextualized text representations from massive corpora.

Information Retrieval (IR) is closely tied to NLP. IR systems process unstructured document collections, rank passages based on similarity scores like TF-IDF or BM25, and extract precise answers for factoid questions.
